Beyond the Code

I grew up between two worlds — Kosovo roots, Swiss upbringing. That probably explains why I'm drawn to things that bridge gaps: between logic and design, between an idea and something you can actually ship.

My father always wanted me to go into IT — so I was in contact with computers from a very early age, and they have played an important role in every chapter of my life.

I started coding at 15 when I joined IMS and picked it up quickly. School gave me the foundation — but the projects I care most about are the ones I built on my own outside of class.

I still have a lot to learn, and I know it. What I bring is the drive to actually do that learning — not just wait to be taught, but go and figure things out.

I ask questions early, communicate when something's unclear, and I don't go quiet when things get hard.

What drives me

I can't leave things half-finished. If something works but looks sloppy or breaks in edge cases, it bothers me until I fix it. I care about what I put my name on.

How I work

I look things up before assuming. I name things clearly. I don't leave TODOs that never get fixed. If I'm unsure, I say so instead of guessing and hoping nobody notices.

Right now

Finishing year 3 at IMS Baden, building Stock Rendite on the side, looking for an internship where I can contribute from day one — not just watch from the sidelines.

Goal

Build products people actually open every day. Not internal tools that exist because someone asked for a dashboard. Somewhere where quality matters.
